## Artwork
Artwork follows the [RetroDualStation artwork standard](https://github.com/Ninte86hl/rocknix-dualstation/blob/main/docs/ARTWORK.md): `romname-hero.png`, `romname-icon.png`, `romname-box.png` and the scraper logo.
The [Artwork Manager](Artwork-Manager) creates the first three; missing images fall back to scraper media.
