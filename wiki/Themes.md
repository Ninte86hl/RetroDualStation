## canvas rds
**canvas rds** is [canvas-ds](https://github.com/toniremi/canvas-ds) by **[toniremi](https://github.com/toniremi)**, adapted with a small overlay
(shared with his permission, CC BY-NC-SA). Top screen: hero image, the video fades in after 5 seconds.

**Grid with info bar:** **Ports → Grid Info** adds a bar below the covers in the grid view with players, name,
year, rating, genre and the button help. Run it again to turn it off.

## dii-ess-aye rds
The second bundled theme: **dii-ess-aye rds** is based on [dii-ess-aye](https://github.com/beebono/dii-ess-aye) by **[beebono](https://github.com/beebono)** – the dual-screen theme for the RG DS that RDS started from (MIT license). Switch in ES under *UI settings → Theme*.

Thank you, toniremi and beebono – both themes keep your names on purpose. 🙏

## Artwork
Artwork follows the [RetroDualStation artwork standard](https://github.com/Ninte86hl/RetroDualStation/blob/main/docs/ARTWORK.md): `romname-hero.png`, `romname-icon.png`, `romname-box.png` and the scraper logo.
The [Artwork Manager](Artwork-Manager) creates the first three; missing images fall back to scraper media.
