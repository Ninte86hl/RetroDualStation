- **RG DS: larger text in ES menus** – a deliberate usability choice: on the smaller 640 × 480 screens ES shows menu text a bit larger than true to scale. Fewer entries fit at once (e.g. scroll to *System settings* and *Quit*), but everything stays easy to read. Control Center, Game switcher and Power menu scale with the screen.
- **No ScreenScraper** in the dual-screen build – the credentials are reserved for the official ROCKNIX build. To scrape, switch to *Single* via Ports and back. (Developer access requested.)
- **RetroAchievements:** *Main menu → RetroAchievements* opens the settings; progress and achievements are on page 3 of the [Control Center](Control-Center).
- **PS1:** the installer sets **pcsx_rearmed** if no working core is selected.
- **DS:** DraStic and DSperate work; RetroArch DS cores are untested.
- **DraStic touch not responding?** Set **Follow 3D Renderer** to *Auto* (also happens on stock ROCKNIX).
- **Back to stock:** *Ports → RGDS Single-Screen ES*; *Uninstall* restores stock ES and theme – games, media, game lists and saves stay untouched.
