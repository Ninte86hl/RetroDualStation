🇩🇪 [Deutsch](Kontrollcenter)

The **Anbernic button** opens a control center on the bottom screen – over ES, RetroArch, DraStic,
DSperate and PortMaster. The game pauses and continues where you left off. Pages: **1** quick settings · **2** game · **3** RetroAchievements.

## Quick settings
- Brightness for both screens, volume, performance, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth
- **Quit game** – no more shortcuts to remember
- **Bottom screen off** – play DS games on one screen; the Anbernic button turns it back on. In RetroArch and PortMaster the bottom screen goes dark automatically.
- **Vibration** – short feedback when opening, switching pages and tapping; switch at the top *(v13.1)*
- **Select** starts the quick tour again

## Game page *(v13)*
For RetroArch, DSperate and DraStic:
- **Save / Load** – 9 slots with preview picture; delete a slot with the bin icon
- **Emulator menu**, **Restart**, **Fast forward**, **Slow motion**, **Screenshot**, **Button layout**, **Quit**

## Same shortcuts everywhere
**Anbernic + R1** – fast forward · **Anbernic + R2 / L2** – save / load · **Anbernic + X** – emulator menu · **Anbernic + Y** – FPS · **Anbernic + Start** – quit · **Anbernic + ◀ / ▶** – change slot

## Power menu
**Power short** – sleep, restart (optionally saving logs), power off, restart ES · **Power long** – sleep immediately

## Notifications on top
New achievements and the volume briefly appear on the top screen over the game.
