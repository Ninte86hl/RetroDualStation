🇩🇪 [Deutsch](Gameswitcher)

**Hold Home** (1.5 s) – recently played games, each with a picture of where you left off, in the shape of its console · **A** – resume exactly there · **Y** – start fresh · **X** – remove from the list · **Home short** – save and back to ES · **Home twice** – straight to the previous game

Works with RetroArch, DraStic, DSperate and PortMaster. Switching takes about 5 seconds –
a loading screen covers both screens and the sound stays muted until you're back in the game.
