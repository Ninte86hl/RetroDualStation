🇩🇪 [Deutsch](RetroAchievements-DE)

Page 3 of the [Control Center](Control-Center) shows your account, the progress of the current game and all its achievements – open one for description, points and rarity *(v13)*.

- Sign in once in ES: **Main menu → RetroAchievements** (the settings open directly; the hint at the top points to the Control Center).
- A new achievement briefly appears on the top screen over the game, with a double vibration pulse.
- Works in games via RetroArch; hardcore and softcore are shown.

### Nintendo DS with DSperate *(v15)*
- DS games in **DSperate** also get page 3 and the unlock notification. They use the same sign-in from ES – nothing extra to set up.
- DSperate unlocks the achievements itself; the Control Center reads game and progress from RetroAchievements and shows new achievements on top.
- RDS turns off DSperate's own pop-ups so no notification appears twice.
- **DraStic** has no RetroAchievements support – choose the DSperate emulator in ES for that.
